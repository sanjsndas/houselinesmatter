Cookies Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2025 · Effective: April 14, 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how House Lines Matter ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website at houselinesmatter.com (the "Site"). It also explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

By continuing to browse or use our Site, you agree to our use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, you should adjust your browser settings or refrain from using the Site.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply information to site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by a domain other than the one you are visiting).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several reasons, including:

  • Essential functionality: To enable core features such as page navigation, secure areas, and remembering your cookie preferences.
  • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with our Site — which pages are most popular, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve our editorial content and user experience.
  • Preferences: To remember choices you make (such as language or region) and provide a more personalized experience.
  • Marketing and advertising: We do not currently serve third-party advertisements. However, if we ever do, cookies may be used to deliver relevant ads and measure their effectiveness.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

The specific cookies we use on the Site are outlined in the table below. We may update this list from time to time as our services evolve.

Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preferences so we don't ask again on every visit. 1 year
session_id Essential Maintains your session while you navigate the Site (e.g., for form submissions). Session
_ga, _gid Analytics (Google Analytics) Used to distinguish users and track site usage anonymously. We use this data to improve content. 2 years / 24 hours
_gat Analytics (Google Analytics) Used to throttle request rate to Google Analytics. 1 minute
pref_lang Preference Remembers your language or region preference (if applicable). 6 months

Note: Third-party cookies (such as those from Google Analytics) are governed by the respective third party's privacy policies. We do not control these cookies directly. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

  • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu. To learn more, visit allaboutcookies.org.
  • Opt-out tools: For analytics cookies, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Our consent banner: If you have previously accepted cookies, you can clear your browser cookies and refresh the page to reset your consent choice.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our consent mechanism because they are necessary for the Site to operate.

5. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may also use various third-party cookies to report usage statistics of the Site. These may include:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand traffic patterns and user behavior. Data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • Embedded content: If we embed content from platforms like YouTube or Vimeo, those services may set their own cookies.

We do not have access to or control over these third-party cookies. You should check the relevant third party's website for more information.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
