    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the most important principle in interior design? +
    Balance and proportion. A space can have beautiful individual pieces but feel wrong if scale and visual weight are off. Start with a floor plan and consider how furniture, light, and negative space interact before choosing finishes.
    How can I make a small room feel bigger? +
    Use consistent flooring throughout, keep window treatments minimal, choose furniture with exposed legs, and paint walls and ceiling in a unified light color. Mirrors help, but thoughtful space planning matters more.
    What lighting temperature is best for living rooms? +
    2700K–3000K (warm white) is ideal for relaxation. Use layered lighting: ambient, task, and accent. Dimmers are essential for flexibility.
    Are open floor plans still popular? +
    Yes, but with more nuance. Many new designs incorporate “broken plan” concepts — partial walls, level changes, and pocket doors to allow separation when needed.
    How do I choose a color palette for my home? +
    Start with a neutral base (white, warm gray, or beige), add one or two accent colors, and use texture to add depth. Test paint samples in different lighting conditions before committing.
