About us

We believe good design
starts with clear lines.

House Lines Matter is an independent editorial publication for interior design, architecture, and the built environment. We publish long-form guides, critical essays, and practical advice — with no fluff, no hidden agendas, and no sponsored content disguised as editorial.

12+ Years of combined editorial experience
200+ Long-form articles published
100% Independent, reader-supported

Why we exist

Most design media is either too superficial or too academic. We started House Lines Matter to fill the gap — a place for thoughtful, accessible writing that respects the intelligence of its readers.

Our editorial is built on three principles: clarity over jargon, substance over trends, and honesty over hype. We don't do listicles for the sake of clicks. We don't publish press releases. We write for people who want to understand how spaces work and why they feel the way they do.

Whether you're a homeowner planning a renovation, an architect looking for critical perspective, or simply someone who loves the built environment — we hope you'll find something useful here.

Meet the editor

Elena Marchetti

Editor-in-Chief & Founder

Elena is an architect and writer based in Milan. She holds a Master's in Architecture from Politecnico di Milano and has over a decade of experience in residential and adaptive-reuse projects across Europe. Before founding House Lines Matter, she worked as a design editor for a leading international architecture magazine.

Her writing focuses on the intersection of material culture, sustainability, and everyday living. She believes that the best design writing should be as considered and intentional as the spaces it describes.

How we work

🔍

Research first

Every article starts with primary sources — interviews, site visits, and technical references. We don't rely on second-hand summaries or AI-generated content.

⚖️

No hidden agendas

We are not sponsored by brands and we don't run native advertising. When we mention a product, it's because we believe it's genuinely relevant — never because we were paid to.

📐

Practical & critical

We aim to be both useful and thoughtful. That means actionable advice grounded in real-world constraints, alongside critical reflection on where design is heading.

Get in touch

We welcome pitches, feedback, and questions. For editorial inquiries, please write to editor@houselinesmatter.com — we read everything, though we can't always respond individually.

Editorial Standards

We follow a strict code of conduct:
— No sponsored content disguised as editorial.
— No affiliate links in critical reviews.
— Corrections are published transparently.
— AI is used only for research assistance, never for writing.
